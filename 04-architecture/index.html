The studio

We believe the most powerful architecture is the kind that makes room for life.

Forma Atelier is a collaborative practice working across architecture, interiors and the places in between. We are drawn to honest materials, considered details and ideas that stand the test of time.

Our team of twelve architects and makers works from London and Copenhagen, bringing different perspectives to one shared ambition: to make thoughtful places.

Our philosophy

A dialogue with
what is already here.

Every site has a story. We begin by listening — to its landscape, its history, and the lives it will hold. What follows is an architecture of response, not imposition.
