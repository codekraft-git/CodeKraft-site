Frequently asked
questions.

Everything you need to know about partnering with CodeKraft and engineering your digital presence.

What services does CodeKraft provide?+

CodeKraft specializes in custom website development, mobile application engineering, UI/UX design, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity hardening for growing businesses.

Can I choose a pre-built website concept and have it tailored to my brand?+

Yes. You can explore our collection of 19 distinct website concepts, save your favorites, and CodeKraft will customize the visual identity, content, features, and architecture specifically for your brand.

How do I contact CodeKraft to start a project?+

You can submit an inquiry through our interactive project form, connect directly on WhatsApp at +91 94007 50981, call us at +91 94007 50981, or email codekraft.pvt@gmail.com.

Are websites engineered by CodeKraft secure and optimized for mobile devices?+

Yes. Every website and digital product is engineered for zero bloat, lightning-fast Core Web Vitals, touch-friendly mobile responsiveness, and hardened security defenses from day one.

What modern technologies does CodeKraft use?+

We build with battle-tested industry standards including Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, AWS, Cloudflare, and Vercel edge infrastructure.
